# Lyric Noir — style guide

## Look
- **Palette:** wall #1E1E1E, sidewalk #0C0C0C, figures 80–165 gray, shadows black at ~80% opacity.
- **One accent color per film**, reserved for the emotional subject (Film 01: red #CD1634 for the woman,
  his scarf, his flower). Police/flash light is cold white, never the accent.
- **Rain always**; never on glass (the subject lives in a different world).
- **Film grain + vignette**, fade in 1.5 s, fade out ~3 s.
- **Shapes:** flat cut-paper figures; the subject can be stylized (Film 01: Cubist-style portrait).

## Storytelling
- The **wall shadows tell the feelings** (dancing together, growing huge, being pulled apart);
  the real figures show the facts.
- The accent color **moves** with the story (it spreads to him in love, drains away at the end).
- One reveal moment per film (a flash, a glitch, a newspaper) where reality breaks in.
- End quiet and lonely: empty frame, pull back in the rain, fade.

## Camera
- One shot per group of lyric lines; **hard cut on a line start**; slow push/drift inside a shot.
- Mix: wide (story), medium (people + shadows), close-up (the subject) — never two close-ups in a row.

## Lyrics on screen
- Neon flicker, word by word, Poppins Regular 60 px, core #FFDEE4 with red glow #FF1E4B.
- Beside the subject (right if there is room, else left), following the camera; no subtitle bar.

## Brand
- Page: **Lyric Noir**. Profile mark: vinyl record + red label + white play button in a neon ring
  (`assets/brand/profile.png`). Bio: "Songs you hear. Stories you see." (+ 2 lines).
- Cover 9:16: kicker "LYRIC NOIR · FILM NO. NN", title (first word italic white, rest bold accent caps),
  artist, a 2-line invented hook, logo. Everything inside the 3:4 grid crop (y 240–1680).
- Captions: question hook + "🎵 Artist – "Song"" + "Animated fan video" + 4–6 hashtags. Never lyrics.
